Inside TikTok's Automated Review Algorithm (OCR & ASR)

TikTok processes millions of new video ads daily using a three-tier automated inspection pipeline before human moderators ever see an ad:

1. Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Every video is sampled at 1–3 frame-per-second intervals. Any on-screen text—including native font stickers, burned-in subtitles, and product packaging labels—is extracted and matched against an international blacklist of banned claims.

2. Automated Speech Recognition (ASR): The audio soundtrack is parsed through ByteDance's speech-to-text models. Even if no words are displayed on screen, speaking phrases like "guaranteed to cure" or "make $5,000 overnight" will trigger an automated rejection within minutes of upload.

3. Destination URL Crawler: TikTok's web crawler fetches your landing page HTML. If the landing page contains before-and-after imagery or promises absent from the ad itself, the ad will still be flagged under Policy 5.1 for "Landing Page Inconsistency".

Best Practices for High-Converting, 100% Compliant Ad Hooks

To maintain high click-through rates (CTR) while staying strictly on the right side of TikTok's automated compliance filters:

Frame Around Experience, Not Guarantee: Shift from claiming universal medical or financial certainty to sharing subjective customer experiences: "Why over 10,000 customers switched to memory foam support..." rather than "Cure your back pain 100% guaranteed".

Include Visible Disclaimers: On financial, SaaS, and fitness landing pages, ensure standard FTC disclaimers are visible in the footer (e.g. "Results may vary depending on individual starting point and consistency").

Avoid Clickbait UI Overlays: Never simulate platform UI buttons (like a fake red notification dot or simulated iPhone alert). TikTok categorizes this as malicious clickbait under Policy 5.4, which can result in immediate domain blacklisting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if an approved ad suddenly gets disapproved?

TikTok periodically re-scans active ads when new policy filters are deployed. If an ad was previously running, check the rejection reason in Ads Manager under the 'Ad Details' tab, edit the flagged text or voiceover, and submit as a new ad creative.

Can I appeal a rejected ad if I believe the bot made a false positive?

Yes. Inside Ads Manager, click 'Appeal' on the rejected ad. In the explanation box, clearly cite why the phrasing complies with policy (for example, citing third-party clinical backing or clarifying that a claim was non-literal). Approximately 35% of false-positive appeals are overturned.

Are Spark Ads held to the same compliance standards as non-Spark ads?

Yes. Even though Spark Ads pull directly from organic TikTok creator posts, once boosted as commercial ads they undergo the exact same automated OCR and ASR advertising compliance filters.
