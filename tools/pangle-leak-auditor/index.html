What Is Pangle & Why Does It Drain DTC E-Commerce Budgets?

Pangle is ByteDance's ad network monetization SDK integrated into tens of thousands of external mobile applications, including hyper-casual games, puzzle games, clean-up tools, and comic readers across Asia, Latin America, Europe, and the Middle East.

When you create a new ad group on TikTok Ads Manager, the placement setting defaults to "Automatic Placement". Because Pangle impressions have significantly lower CPMs than the native TikTok video feed, TikTok's automated pacing algorithm frequently allocates 30% to 65% of your daily budget to Pangle to achieve lower average blended CPM metrics.

Step-by-Step: How to Completely Exclude Pangle from TikTok Ads

Disabling Pangle requires less than 30 seconds inside TikTok Ads Manager. Follow this exact workflow:

Step 1: Open Your Ad Group Settings. Navigate to your campaign and click "Edit" on the specific Ad Group you want to optimize.

Step 2: Locate the Placements Module. Scroll down to the second section titled "Placements". By default, "Automatic Placement (Recommended)" will be pre-selected.

Step 3: Select "Select Placement" (Manual). Click on the manual placement radio button. A list of available channels will expand:

  • ☑️ TikTok (Keep Checked)
  • ⬜ Pangle (Uncheck this box)

Step 4: Turn Off User Comments & Video Downloads (Optional). In the advanced settings below placements, we also recommend reviewing Video Downloads if you want to protect your unique creative hooks from competitor ripping.

Step 5: Save & Publish. Within 1 hour, your campaign's traffic will re-route exclusively to genuine TikTok feed users, cutting wasted spend instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will disabling Pangle increase my CPM?

Yes, your reported blended CPM will likely increase by 20% to 50%. However, because 100% of impressions are now delivered to real, attentive TikTok users rather than bot-heavy casual mobile apps, your actual Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) and ROAS will improve significantly.

Can I disable Pangle on active Smart Performance Campaigns (SPC)?

TikTok's automated Smart Performance Campaigns (SPC) do not allow manual placement exclusions. To avoid Pangle, you must set up standard Custom Campaigns where Manual Placement selection is unlocked.

Can I use Block Lists on Pangle instead of disabling it completely?

TikTok provides a Pangle Block List feature where you can upload app package IDs. However, with millions of rotating casual apps on Google Play and the App Store, maintaining an effective block list is nearly impossible for non-enterprise advertisers. Turning Pangle off entirely is the safest approach.
