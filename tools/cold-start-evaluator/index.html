Why Do TikTok Ads Stop Spending or Get 0 Impressions?

Unlike Meta Ads, which frequently spends full daily budgets regardless of bid performance, TikTok's advertising auction engine operates on a strict eCPM (effective Cost Per Mille) pacing threshold:

eCPM = Bid × Estimated Click-Through Rate (eCTR) × Estimated Conversion Rate (eCVR) × 1,000

When an ad group is newly created, it has zero historical conversion data. The auction algorithm has no empirical proof of your ad's eCTR or eCVR. If your target CPA (in Cost Cap) or daily budget is too restrictive, the calculated eCPM drops below the auction floor of competing advertisers, and TikTok's bid engine automatically bids $0—resulting in 0 impressions.

The 50-Event Learning Phase Rule Explained

TikTok's delivery algorithm requires approximately 50 completed optimization events within 7 continuous days per ad group to successfully exit the initial learning phase.

Target Event Avg. Industry CPA Minimum Daily Budget (5× CPA) 7-Day Required Spend to Exit Learning Phase
Complete Payment (Purchase) $20 – $35 $100 – $175 / day $1,000 – $1,750 USD
Initiate Checkout $8 – $15 $40 – $75 / day $400 – $750 USD
Add to Cart $4 – $8 $20 – $40 / day $200 – $400 USD
Lead Form Submission $6 – $14 $30 – $70 / day $300 – $700 USD

Strategic Takeaway: If you have a budget under $50/day and sell a high-ticket item with a $40 CPA, optimizing directly for Complete Payment will cause the ad group to stay perpetually trapped in the learning phase. In this case, either increase daily budget or optimize for an upper-funnel event (such as Initiate Checkout) during week 1.

Actionable Checklist: How to Kickstart a Frozen Ad Group

If your campaign has delivered 0 impressions for over 12 hours after passing review, follow this sequential troubleshooting protocol:

Step 1: Switch Bidding to Lowest Cost. If you are running Cost Cap, duplicate the ad group and switch to Lowest Cost. Let TikTok gather initial impression velocity for 24–48 hours before applying bid ceilings.

Step 2: Check Daily Budget Multiplier. Ensure daily ad group budget is at least 5× your expected CPA. If CPA is $25, budget must be ≥ $125/day.

Step 3: Broaden Targeting & Turn Off Pangle. Remove tight interest layering or micro-geographic boundaries. Keep targeting broad (age, gender, country) and isolate placements strictly to the TikTok feed.

Step 4: Check Account Daily Spend Cap. Unverified consumer TikTok accounts often have an automated $50/day global spend cap applied by the risk engine. Check your Business Center verification status or upgrade via partner whitelisted credits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does TikTok's ad review process typically take?

Most ads pass automated review within 2 to 4 hours. However, if your ad mentions sensitive keywords (medical, financial, or branded names), it enters manual queue which may take up to 24 hours.

What does "Learning Phase Failed" mean in TikTok Ads Manager?

It means the ad group failed to generate 50 conversions within 7 days. While the ad will continue to deliver, CPA and CPM volatility will be higher. The recommended action is testing fresh creative hooks with a higher budget.

Should I edit an active ad group during the learning phase?

No. Significant edits (altering targeting, changing creative, or adjusting budget by more than 20% in a single day) will reset the learning phase back to 0 conversions.
