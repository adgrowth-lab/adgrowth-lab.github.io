## ⚡ The 3-Tier Automated Ad Review Architecture

When you submit an ad on TikTok Ads Manager, it does **not** immediately go to a human reviewer. It is processed by ByteDance's automated multi-modal safety filter across three parallel tiers:

```
[ Ad Video & Text Upload ]
          │
          ├──► Tier 1: OCR Computer Vision (Scans all on-screen text, banners, stickers)
          ├──► Tier 2: Speech-to-Text / Whisper Transcription (Transcribes spoken voiceover & lyrics)
          └──► Tier 3: Headless Destination Crawler (Renders destination URL, checks claim consistency & legal footer)
          │
          ▼
   [ Risk Scoring Engine ]
   ├── Risk Score < 15:  ✅ Instant Automated Approval (Typically 5–15 mins)
   ├── Risk Score 15-50: ⚠️ Escalated to Level-1 Human Review Queue (2–12 hours)
   └── Risk Score > 50:  ❌ Instant Rejection / Potential Account Probation Flag
```

If an ad group receives multiple automated rejections within a 48-hour window, the **Entire Ad Account** is automatically placed in an internal risk sandbox ($50 daily spend cap or temporary suspension).

## 🚫 The 7 High-Risk Policy Categories & Banned Terms

### 1. Absolute Superlatives & Guaranteed Outcomes (Policy 5.1)
TikTok strictly prohibits claims that guarantee specific, universal, or permanent physical or financial results without independent scientific substantiation.

* **Trigger Words / Phrases**:
  * ❌ `100% guaranteed`, `guaranteed results`, `guaranteed cure`
  * ❌ `miracle cure`, `miracle pill`, `miracle cream`, `magic solution`
  * ❌ `permanent fix`, `permanent cure`, `works for everyone`
  * ❌ `zero effort`, `best in the world`, `risk-free guarantee`
* **Why It Flags**: Triggers the algorithmic exaggeration filter. TikTok requires all performance claims to be qualified by individual experience.
* **Compliant Safe Alternatives**:
  * ✅ *"Formulated to help support..."*
  * ✅ *"A customer favorite designed for everyday routines."*
  * ✅ *"Over 10,000 customers have shared their positive feedback."*
  * ✅ *"Backed by a 30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee."*

---

### 2. Rapid Weight Loss & Body Shaming (Policy 5.2)
TikTok maintains the strictest body image advertising standards among all social platforms, especially to protect users under 18.

* **Trigger Words / Phrases**:
  * ❌ `lose 20 lbs in 2 weeks`, `lose belly fat fast`, `melt fat overnight`
  * ❌ `burn fat while you sleep`, `skinny tea`, `diet pills`
  * ❌ `before and after`, `before & after` (both verbal and visual split-screen)
  * ❌ `stop being fat`, `ugly cellulite`, `target stubborn fat`
* **Why It Flags**: Immediate CRITICAL rejection. Prompts severe account-level penalties. Split-screen before/after body images are banned across video and landing pages.
* **Compliant Safe Alternatives**:
  * ✅ *"Support your active lifestyle and daily wellness habits."*
  * ✅ *"Packed with clean electrolytes and natural botanical energy."*
  * ✅ *"How I stay energized during my morning workouts."*

---

### 3. Deceptive Financial Schemes & "Get Rich Quick" (Policy 5.3)
TikTok's anti-fraud algorithm aggressively terminates accounts promoting speculative investment, passive income secrets, or unrealistic earnings.

* **Trigger Words / Phrases**:
  * ❌ `make $5,000 a day`, `make money while you sleep`, `get rich quick`
  * ❌ `passive income secret`, `financial freedom in 7 days`
  * ❌ `crypto glitch`, `unlimited cash hack`, `guaranteed profits`
  * ❌ `turn $100 into $1,000 overnight`, `secret money system`
* **Why It Flags**: Permanent Business Center suspension. TikTok classifies promissory financial claims as predatory consumer fraud.
* **Compliant Safe Alternatives**:
  * ✅ *"Learn the core fundamentals of digital marketing."*
  * ✅ *"Workflow tools built to streamline your freelance business."*
  * ✅ *"Practical accounting strategies for independent creators."*

---

### 4. Clickbait, Deceptive System UI & Fake Censorship (Policy 5.4)
Attempting to create artificial urgency by pretending TikTok or authorities are censoring the video triggers an instant algorithmic demotion.

* **Trigger Words / Phrases**:
  * ❌ `tiktok banned this`, `they don't want you to know this`
  * ❌ `watch before it's deleted`, `secret hack they hid from you`
  * ❌ `system warning: tap here`, `your account will be deleted`
  * ❌ `free iPhone for the next 10 viewers`, `glitch in the system`
* **Why It Flags**: Violates platform integrity guidelines. Videos simulating native UI warnings or countdown clocks are removed automatically.
* **Compliant Safe Alternatives**:
  * ✅ *"Here is an unexpected tip that changed how I organize my desk."*
  * ✅ *"3 features most people overlook in their editing software."*
  * ✅ *"A breakdown of why this workflow saves 2 hours a week."*

---

### 5. Intellectual Property, Replicas & Designer Dupes (Policy 3.1)
Counterfeiting and unauthorized brand affiliation trigger instant, non-negotiable permanent bans.

* **Trigger Words / Phrases**:
  * ❌ `1:1 replica`, `fake rolex`, `exact clone`, `knockoff`
  * ❌ `designer dupe`, `rep shoes`, `rep sneakers`, `rep bags`
  * ❌ `unauthorized batch`, `mirror quality`, `factory outlet gucci`
* **Why It Flags**: ByteDance maintains automated image recognition and keyword matching for over 5,000 global luxury and trademarked brands.
* **Compliant Safe Alternatives**:
  * ✅ Market original, proprietary brand aesthetics.
  * ✅ Highlight specific materials (e.g., *"Full-grain Italian leather minimalist wallet"*).
  * ✅ Never invoke luxury brand trademarks in hashtags, audio, or caption tags.

---

### 6. Adult, Sensual & Libido Claims (Policy 4.1)
* **Trigger Words / Phrases**:
  * ❌ `boost libido`, `enhance performance in bed`, `viagra alternative`
  * ❌ `sex drive booster`, `male enhancement`, `hot singles in your area`
  * ❌ `adults only 18+`, `nsfw`
* **Why It Flags**: Zero tolerance for adult health or enhancement products on the self-serve ad network.

---

### 7. Non-Certified Medical & Therapeutic Claims (Policy 5.5)
* **Trigger Words / Phrases**:
  * ❌ `cures acne`, `treats clinical depression`, `replaces prescription`
  * ❌ `heals arthritis pain`, `erases wrinkles in minutes`, `reverses aging`
* **Compliant Safe Alternatives**:
  * ✅ *"Formulated with hyaluronic acid to deeply hydrate dry skin."*
  * ✅ *"Helps reduce the appearance of surface redness."*
  * ✅ *"Gentle daily skincare for sensitive complexions."*

The `audit.py` engine includes a built-in compliance scanner that analyzes raw copy, video transcripts, or text files against all 7 policy pillars:


## ✅ Pre-Launch Creative Checklist

Before publishing any ad group, ensure:
1. **No Absolute Superlatives**: Removed all instances of "100%", "miracle", "permanent", "cure".
2. **Audio Transcription Check**: Run your audio file through whisper or speech-to-text to ensure no banned words are spoken in the background track.
3. **Landing Page Consistency**: Your Shopify/custom lander headline must not make claims bolder than your ad video.
4. **Transparent Footer**: Landing page contains working links to:
   * Privacy Policy
   * Terms of Service
   * Shipping Policy
   * 30-Day Refund & Return Policy
   * Full Company Contact Address & Support Email
