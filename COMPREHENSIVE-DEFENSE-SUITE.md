## 🧭 3. The 30-Day Operational SOP (From Setup to Scale)

### Phase 1: Days 1–3 — Foundation & Payment Fortification
1. **Set Up Tier-1 Commercial Banking**:
   * Use **Mercury** (`405081`), **Relay Financial** (`436152`), or **Stripe Treasury** (`414328`).
   * Never bind anonymous prepaid VCCs (PST.net, Capitalist) to fresh Business Centers.
2. **AVS Alignment**:
   * Set Cardholder Name to your exact Business Center legal name.
   * Enter your bank statement billing address down to the exact ZIP code.
   * Fund card with at least $100.00 prior to adding to Ads Manager.
3. **Verify Business Entity**:
   * Complete Advertiser Verification (KYB) under Business Center settings with your government registry extract and EIN/VAT certificate.

### Phase 2: Days 4–7 — Creative Pre-Flight & Cold-Start Launch
1. **Lint All Creative Scripts**:
   * Run `python3 audit.py compliance --file script.txt` on all voiceover transcripts and on-screen text.
   * Strip out "100%", "cure", "belly fat", "before and after", "make $X", and counterfeit brand terms.
2. **First 1.5-Second Hook Rule**:
   * Ensure video opens with a human face looking directly into the camera or an instant visual disruption.
   * Sound must be ON with human voiceover or commercial trending music.
3. **Cold-Start Budgeting**:
   * Set daily ad group budget to at least **10x your target CPA** (e.g. $25 target CPA ➔ $250/day budget).
   * Optimize exclusively for **Complete Payment / Purchase**. Never "season" with Traffic or Add to Cart.
   * Choose **Manual Placements** and **UNCHECK Pangle**.

### Phase 3: Days 8–14 — Traffic Audit & Scaling
1. **Audit for Pangle Leakage**:
   * Run `python3 audit.py pangle --csv shopify_traffic.csv` after the first $500 of spend.
   * Confirm that non-TikTok referrer sessions represent <5% of traffic.
2. **Creative Rotation (3x3 Matrix)**:
   * When winning angles fatigue after 48–72 hours, rotate in 3 new opening hooks against the proven video body.
   * Scale budget via CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) across 3–5 ad groups to protect pixel learning stability.

### Phase 4: Days 15–30 — Spend-Match Redemption & Enterprise Expansion
1. **Claim Partner Matching Credits**:
   * Route ad account onboarding via the official TikTok Partner portal:  
     👉 `https://getstartedtiktok.partnerlinks.io/apmgdhnv6eng`
   * Qualify for spend match tiers:
     * Spend $200 ➔ Get $200 (100% Match)
     * Spend $1,000 ➔ Get $750 (75% Match)
     * Spend $4,000 ➔ Get $3,000 (75% Match)
     * Spend $10,000 ➔ Get $6,000 (Maximum Cap)
2. **Emergency Protocol (If Suspended)**:
   * Immediately run `python3 audit.py appeal --type [unusual-activity|payment|creative]` to generate a customized 4-Pillar appeal packet.
   * Follow the 24-hour escalation protocol outlined in [`appeal-templates/README.md`](appeal-templates/README.md).
