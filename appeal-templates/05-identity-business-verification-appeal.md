# Appeal Template 05: Advertiser Identity Verification (KYB/KYC) Appeal

**Best Used For**: Accounts that failed the mandatory TikTok Advertiser Verification (Business Verification) due to name abbreviations, DBA differences, or address mismatches on official filings.

---

### Ticket Metadata
* **Subject**: KYB RE-VERIFICATION APPEAL: Business Entity Documentation - [YOUR_LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME] (Account ID: [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID])
* **Category**: Advertiser Identity Verification (KYB/KYC)
* **Target Escalation**: Global Identity & Business Verification Compliance

---

### Mandatory Document Attachments
1. `[Doc 1]` Official Government Certificate of Incorporation / Business Registry Extract (Full color, unedited).
2. `[Doc 2]` Official Tax Authority Filing: IRS Form CP-575 / EIN Letter (US), VAT Certificate (UK/EU), or national tax certificate.
3. `[Doc 3]` Government Photo ID of Primary Director / Legal Representative (Passport or National ID card).
4. `[Doc 4]` Corporate Bank Statement or Official Utility Bill verifying registered company physical address.

---
