# Appeal Template 03: Creative Policy & Misleading Claims Rectification Appeal

**Best Used For**: Account suspensions triggered by repeated ad disapprovals under Policy 5.1 (Misleading Claims/Exaggerations), Policy 5.2 (Body Shaming/Weight Loss), or Policy 5.4 (Clickbait).

---

### Ticket Metadata
* **Subject**: POLICY COMPLIANCE APPEAL: Creative Review & Account Reinstatement - [YOUR_BRAND_NAME] (Account ID: [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID])
* **Category**: Ad Policy Enforcement & Creative Compliance
* **Target Escalation**: Content Integrity & Ad Standards Escalation

---

### Mandatory Document Attachments
1. `[Doc 1]` Full copy transcript of the flagged ad with line-by-line remediation notes.
2. `[Doc 2]` Third-party quality certifications, laboratory testing, or ingredient documentation proving product authenticity.
3. `[Doc 3]` Clean, revised video creative asset ready to replace the disapproved ad.
4. `[Doc 4]` Screenshots of destination landing page showing working links to Privacy Policy, Terms, and 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee.

---

### Copy-Paste Appeal Letter

```text
Dear TikTok Advertising Policy & Content Integrity Team,

We respectfully appeal the recent ad creative disapproval and resultant account suspension on:
• Business / Brand Name: [YOUR_BRAND_NAME]
• Ad Account ID: [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID]
• Store URL: [YOUR_WEBSITE_URL]
• Ticket ID: [YOUR_TICKET_ID]
• Date: [CURRENT_DATE]

1. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF FLAGGED CONTENT
We have reviewed the notice citing Policy 5.1 (Misleading Claims / Exaggerations) regarding our recent ad group. We recognize that certain colloquial phrasing in our video hook could be interpreted by automated systems as an over-promissory or absolute claim.

We take TikTok's Community Guidelines and Ad Policies with utmost seriousness and acknowledge that our previous phrasing did not meet your high standards of creative transparency.

2. IMMEDIATE REMEDIATION & CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
We have taken immediate, comprehensive corrective measures:
• Completely deleted the flagged creative assets from all campaigns in Account [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID].
• Conducted an exhaustive audit of our entire creative catalog using a strict compliance linter to eliminate all absolute superlatives.
• Re-edited the creative assets to focus strictly on real customer experience and objective product utility without superlatives, before/after claims, or medical promises.
• Updated our destination landing page ([YOUR_WEBSITE_URL]) to feature clear disclaimers, transparent refund policies (30-day money-back guarantee), and verified customer testimonials.

3. SUPPORTING PRODUCT CREDENTIALS
To verify our product claims are truthful and non-deceptive, we have attached:
[Attachment 1] Independent Quality Certifications / Lab Test Reports
[Attachment 2] Comprehensive Ingredient / Material Safety Data Sheet
[Attachment 3] Customer Support & Return Metrics proving >96% positive delivery rate

4. REQUEST FOR ACCOUNT REINSTATEMENT
As an established e-commerce brand investing significant resources into the TikTok ecosystem, our objective is to run compliant, high-converting campaigns that delight TikTok users. We respectfully request that you restore Account [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID] to active status so we may submit our fully compliant creative assets for review.

Sincerely,
Creative Operations & Legal Compliance Lead
[YOUR_BRAND_NAME]
```
