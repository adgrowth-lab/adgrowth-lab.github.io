# Appeal Template 04: Business Center (BC) & Agency Account Ban Appeal

**Best Used For**: Master Business Center (BC) suspensions, agency multi-account portfolio bans, or when multiple client ad accounts are disabled due to shared linkage.

---

### Ticket Metadata
* **Subject**: EXECUTIVE APPEAL: Business Center Account Suspension Review - [AGENCY/ORGANIZATION_NAME] (BC ID: [YOUR_BC_ID])
* **Category**: Business Center Management & Agency Operations
* **Target Escalation**: Global Agency & Enterprise Risk Management

---

### Mandatory Document Attachments
1. `[Doc 1]` Master Business Center Registration Documents & Corporate Certificate of Incorporation.
2. `[Doc 2]` Government-issued identification of Primary Business Center Administrators.
3. `[Doc 3]` Client Representation Contracts or Authorization Letters confirming agency authority.
4. `[Doc 4]` Complete internal audit log of all linked sub-accounts with remediation status.

---

### Copy-Paste Appeal Letter

```text
Dear TikTok Global Agency & Enterprise Risk Management,

We are submitting an executive appeal regarding the suspension of our TikTok Business Center:
• Organization / Agency Name: [YOUR_ORGANIZATION_NAME]
• Business Center ID: [YOUR_BC_ID]
• Registered Website: [YOUR_WEBSITE_URL]
• Reference Case: [YOUR_TICKET_ID]
• Date: [CURRENT_DATE]

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & IMPACT
Our Business Center (BC ID: [YOUR_BC_ID]) was recently disabled. This action has halted legitimate commercial advertising across our client accounts and DTC brands, resulting in substantial financial disruption. 

Our compliance team has conducted an exhaustive portfolio review across all linked sub-accounts to isolate the root cause.

2. PORTFOLIO AUDIT FINDINGS
The automated flag appears to have originated from:
- A legacy sandbox test account that experienced an automated billing decline.
- Rapid client onboarding resulting in multiple sub-account activations over a compressed timeframe.

We confirm that all active brands operating under this Business Center are verified commercial entities with legitimate supply chains and valid payment instruments.

3. ENTERPRISE COMPLIANCE MEASURES
To ensure absolute platform harmony, we have implemented the following governance protocols:
• Unlinked and archived all inactive or test ad accounts.
• Implemented a strict pre-flight compliance checklist (zero tolerance for misleading claims or unauthorized placements).
• Consolidated billing exclusively to tier-1 commercial banking partners with pre-funded reserves.

[Attached Documentation]
- Official Enterprise Incorporation Documents
- Identification Records of Primary BC Administrators
- Client Representation Authorizations
- Financial Solvency & Good Standing Certification

4. RESOLUTION REQUEST
We value our commercial partnership with TikTok and request an expedited executive re-examination to reactivate Business Center [YOUR_BC_ID] and its constituent compliant ad accounts.

Sincerely,
Managing Partner & Head of Ad Operations
[YOUR_ORGANIZATION_NAME]
Corporate Contact: [YOUR_BUSINESS_EMAIL]
```
