# Appeal Template 02: Payment Discrepancy & Card Verification Appeal

**Best Used For**: Accounts suspended immediately upon adding a credit/debit card, card authorization hold failure ($1-$10 hold decline), or billing address/name mismatch.

---

### Ticket Metadata
* **Subject**: PAYMENT VERIFICATION APPEAL: Card Discrepancy Reversal - [YOUR_BRAND_NAME] (Account ID: [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID])
* **Category**: Billing & Payment Security
* **Target Escalation**: Anti-Fraud Operations & Billing Operations

---

### Mandatory Document Attachments
1. `[Doc 1]` Official Bank Statement for the card on file (dated within last 30 days) showing company name, registered address, and recent TikTok auth holds.
2. `[Doc 2]` High-resolution photo or digital screenshot of the card showing **Cardholder Name** and the **Last 4 Digits** (Middle 12 digits and CVV strictly redacted for security).
3. `[Doc 3]` Corporate resolution or document linking the Cardholder Name to [YOUR_BRAND_NAME].
4. `[Doc 4]` Certificate of Good Standing / Business Incorporation.

---

### Copy-Paste Appeal Letter

```text
Dear TikTok Ads Billing & Anti-Fraud Operations Team,

We are submitting this official verification appeal regarding the payment suspension on our account:
• Account Name: [YOUR_BRAND_NAME]
• Ad Account ID: [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID]
• Case / Ticket Reference: [YOUR_TICKET_ID]
• Date of Submission: [CURRENT_DATE]

1. BACKGROUND & ROOT CAUSE EXPLANATION
Our account was flagged for a payment discrepancy or unverified payment method upon binding our commercial payment card. We understand TikTok's strict security protocols against automated payment fraud. 

This verification flag occurred due to:
- Using our corporate commercial debit card issued by our business banking partner (e.g. Mercury / Stripe / Relay).
- An initial AVS (Address Verification Service) formatting discrepancy between our bank statement address and the Ads Manager billing profile.

2. CARD OWNERSHIP & FINANCIAL PROOF
We confirm that the card added to Ads Account [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID] is 100% legally owned and authorized by [YOUR_BRAND_NAME]. To substantiate this, we have provided comprehensive evidence:
[Attachment 1] Official Corporate Bank Statement (last 30 days) displaying:
  • Company Legal Name and Registered Corporate Address
  • Bank Name & Official Financial Institution Header
  • Exact matching ending digits of the payment method (Ending: [LAST_4_DIGITS])
  • Recent test authorization / pending charges from TikTok Ads
[Attachment 2] Card Verification Image confirming our authorized director is the legal cardholder (all sensitive CVV and middle digits redacted per PCI compliance).
[Attachment 3] Certificate of Good Standing / Corporate Entity Documentation proving company authority.

3. RECTIFICATION ACTIONS TAKEN
We have verified that:
• The billing address in TikTok Ads Manager now perfectly mirrors the exact format on our bank statement.
• Sufficient liquid capital ($5,000+ USD equivalent) is maintained to cover all billing thresholds seamlessly.
• 3D-Secure mobile authentication is enabled and will be immediately approved for all transactions.

4. CONCLUSION & RESTORATION REQUEST
All financial documentation demonstrates legitimate corporate ownership with zero fraud risk. We kindly request that you verify the attached records, lift the payment hold, and reactivate Account [YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID].

Respectfully submitted,
Head of Finance & Operations
[YOUR_BRAND_NAME]
Official Corporate Email: [YOUR_BUSINESS_EMAIL]
```
